Website ownership

Your Business. Your Website. Your Control.

After you meet the payment obligations in your agreement, you can request a transferable package for eligible website work. We’ll document what’s included so you know what another provider can take over.

What can transfer

Custom website source, client-owned assets, applicable data exports, and deployment documentation.

What stays separate

Passwords and other secrets, Devco’s shared proprietary infrastructure, and licenses that can’t be transferred.

Search visibility & site health

Regular SEO monitoring is included at no extra cost with website maintenance. We check whether Google can find your pages and look for problems that could make it harder for customers to discover or use your site.
